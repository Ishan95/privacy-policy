Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Disclaimer: This application is an independent, non-governmental platform designed solely to help government hospital nurses find mutual job transfers. It is a personal application and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government authority or health ministry.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use our application, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, verified email address, date of birth, and account credentials.
  • Professional & Transfer Details: Current hospital/office location, grade/rank, current district, and preferred transfer districts (1st, 2nd, and 3rd choices).
  • Contact Details: Phone number (optional display) and messaging logs within the app.
  • Device & Usage Data: Push notification tokens to alert you when matching transfer candidates join.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to facilitate mutual transfer connections between users:

  • To verify your identity via email verification upon registration.
  • To match your transfer choices (preferred districts) with other users looking for mutual transfers.
  • To send you instant push notifications when a user matching your transfer preferences joins the app.
  • To enable direct, secure in-app messaging between users looking to discuss transfers.

3. User Privacy & Profile Visibility Controls

We respect your privacy and give you control over what other users can see:

  • Visibility Controls: You can choose to hide your phone number and specific office/hospital location from public view on your profile settings.
  • Public Information: Other registered users can view your name, grade, and transfer preferences to determine transfer compatibility.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Your information is shared only within the app with other registered users for the sole purpose of finding a mutual transfer match.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical security measures (including secure database authentication and encrypted connections) to protect your account and personal details against unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. You can delete your account directly through the app settings menu or by contacting support at gmidbandara@gmail.com.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
